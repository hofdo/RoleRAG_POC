# The Archduchy of Sarn

Sarn is a small and stubbornly independent archduchy wedged between greater powers, and it endures for one reason: beneath its hills run the richest veins of magic-crystal in the known lands. The crystals power wards, forges, and far-speaking, and every empire and free city needs them. All of them must deal with Sarn to get them.

The crystal trade has made Sarnhold Keep a center of magic and commerce far beyond its size. Mage-wrights come to study the raw stone, merchants come to bargain, and envoys come to flatter. The Archduke holds the only keys to the deep mines and guards them jealously.

The present Duke of Sarn is young but already feared. He is a mage of rare strength and a swordsman who has never been beaten in the dueling-yard, and he won his border war against the river-lords before his twentieth year. Men speak of his temper in low voices and call him the Crystal Wolf behind his back.

House Varn is one of the great houses of the empire, old and proud and short of coin. It has long coveted a steady supply of Sarn's crystals, and has at last secured a marriage-alliance: a daughter of Duke Corvane of Varn, sent west to wed the Crystal Wolf and bind the two houses together.
