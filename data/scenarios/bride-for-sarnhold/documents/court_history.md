# The Sarn Alliance and the Houses

For three generations the Dukes of Sarn have kept their independence by playing the great powers against one another, selling crystals to all and loyalty to none. The marriage with House Varn is the boldest binding any Sarn duke has risked, and the court is uneasy about tying the archduchy to a single imperial house.

The empire to which House Varn belongs is vast, hungry, and riven by quiet feuds between its great houses. Duke Corvane of Varn is known for his pride, for his cruelty to those who fail him, and for keeping his children on a short and bitter leash.

The young Duke of Sarn came to his seat after his father fell in the river war. He has ruled alone since, trusting few, and showed little interest in marriage until the alliance with Varn made it a matter of state. The court quietly hopes the match will soften him.
