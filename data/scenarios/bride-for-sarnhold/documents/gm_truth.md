# GM ONLY — The Truth Behind the Marriage

Lady Iseult is not the spoiled heiress the court expects. Her father, Duke Corvane, neglected and beat her for years and kept her isolated. He sent her into this marriage not as a prize but as a discard, because she was the daughter he could most afford to lose, after she refused his order to spy on the Duke of Sarn from inside the marriage.

Iseult arrives braced for another cruel master and resolved to feel nothing. Her handmaid Nessa has witnessed the worst of the abuse and guards it fiercely, and carries an unsent letter in which Iseult begged for rescue. Chancellor Bram learned from a courier that the bride was reported "damaged goods" and buried the report to protect the crystal alliance. The Duke knows none of this.
